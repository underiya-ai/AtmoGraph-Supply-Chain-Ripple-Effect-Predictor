# AtmoGraph – Supply Chain Ripple Effect Prediction

## 📌 Project Overview

AtmoGraph is a data-driven project focused on analyzing and predicting ripple effects in supply chains. It aims to understand how disruptions in one part of the supply chain can impact inventory, product demand, suppliers, delivery timelines, and overall operations.

The project focuses on data analysis, machine learning, and predictive approaches to identify potential risks and improve supply chain visibility.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze supply chain datasets and identify meaningful patterns.
- Understand stock levels, product demand, and supplier lead times.
- Identify potential supply chain disruptions.
- Apply data analysis and machine learning techniques.
- Develop insights that can support better supply chain decisions.
